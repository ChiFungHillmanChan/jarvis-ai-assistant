JARVIS v0.5.15

This update tidies Settings > Voice: the voice lists no longer repeat names, every voice shows its accent and whether it is female or male, and you can play a sample and change the speaking speed.

New
- Play sample: each voice has a Play sample button that reads one fixed sentence, so you can hear a voice before you choose it.
- Speaking speed: choose Slow, Normal or Fast.

Changed
- The Read replies aloud switch now sits above the voices. Turned off, JARVIS stays silent and the voice, sample and speed controls are greyed out. Volume follows your Mac volume.
- The English list shows only English voices and the Cantonese list only Cantonese voices, each once, with accent and gender, for example "Samantha (American English, female)". Sound-effect and old-style voices are no longer listed; if you already use one, your setting is kept.
- Keep recordings moves to its own Recordings group.

Fixed
- The voice list used to show each language's version of a voice under the same name (Eddy appeared 14 times) and cut names such as "Bad News" short.
